# Security

## Report a vulnerability

Email [security@emojisense.com](mailto:security@emojisense.com). Do not open a public issue.

Include:

- the package, app or URL, and its version
- the steps to reproduce the problem, or a proof of concept
- the impact that you expect

We confirm that we received the report and keep you informed until the fix is released. Follow the
rules in "Security research" of the [Acceptable Use Policy](https://emojisense.com/legal/acceptable-use/).

## Scope

- The npm packages `emojisense` and `@emojisense/*`
- The hosted service at emojisense.com and its subdomains (website, API, dashboard)
- The Chrome extension, the Raycast extension, the WordPress plugin, the Discourse theme component
  and the Swift package

## Supported versions

Only the latest release of each package and app gets security fixes.
